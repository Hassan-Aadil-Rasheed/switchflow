# SwitchFlow: demo video under 3 minutes

Record the real Switch room and SwitchFlow app. Use a connected chat group with actual agent messages. Hide credentials, private IDs, and personal details. Keep the final edit below 3:00 and upload it as public or unlisted to YouTube or Loom.

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem

**Show:** SwitchFlow overview.

**Say:** “Small teams have requests arriving in chat, but the evidence, AI recommendation, owner decision, and result often get separated. SwitchFlow brings those steps into one workflow connected through Switch.”

## 0:20–0:50 — Setup

**Show:** Connected Switch account and chat destination, selected CSV, workflow brief.

**Say:** “I connect my chat group through Switch, upload a business file, describe the goal, and select which data the agents may inspect. Here I’m using a restaurant capacity sheet.”

## 0:50–1:20 — Real agents in chat

**Show:** Customer request in the actual chat room, intake response, specialist and analyst messages. Zoom on the agent names and source file evidence.

**Say:** “A customer asks for cookies in the group. Switch routes that message to the team. The agents check the selected capacity record and state what is still unknown. They do not accept the order for me.”

## 1:20–2:15 — Owner decision

**Show:** SwitchFlow Approvals card with the customer, quantity, recorded capacity, and missing checks. Make one real decision. Show the corresponding chat reply and, if accepted, the CSV's committed count after reservation.

**Say:** “The decision comes to me in Approvals. The file shows recorded capacity, but I still need to check ingredients, time, staffing, and other commitments. I choose whether to accept or decline. SwitchFlow records my decision and replies in the original customer thread. On acceptance, it also reserves the amount in the local CSV.”

## 2:15–2:45 — Switch and limits

**Show:** Switch room handoffs, then the app's room history or verifier result if available.

**Say:** “Switch provides the shared room, agent identities, and message routing. SwitchFlow adds the business brief, selected data, approval screen, and decision record. This is a real chat workflow with a human decision; supplier purchasing, payment, and delivery are outside this demo.”

Leave a few seconds for the final frame. If the real outcome is a decline, show that outcome and omit the CSV reservation claim from the narration for that run.

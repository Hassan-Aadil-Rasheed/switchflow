# Chats in SwitchFlow

Connect Telegram, Discord, Slack, Microsoft Teams, and Mattermost separately in **Connections**. The status count comes from the signed-in Switch server. Having credentials ready does not mean an app is connected; each setup must be saved and validated.

In **Chats**, choose a messaging app, then a named channel, then a customer's conversation. The header identifies the app, connected account, channel, and person. A channel can contain several customers with identical display names; conversation and destination IDs keep those threads distinct.

Write a reply and check **Send to** before choosing **Send reply**. Replies go to the selected thread in its original channel. They are never broadcast to other apps. To address everyone in a channel instead, explicitly choose **Channel activity**.

The inbox shows channels the current Switch account is allowed to read. Refresh chats to load recent messages; the latest 200 messages per channel are available here. Older history remains in the messaging app. Unconfirmed replies retain their message reference when retried.

Customer order workflows remain separate from manual chats: customer request → acknowledgement → capacity evidence → owner's approval → reservation and reply. The owner approves from SwitchFlow. Replying manually does not approve a pending order.

Keep the laptop, SwitchFlow, Switch server, and the selected platform's connection running. Connecting a bot does not grant access to a person's entire private messaging account.
